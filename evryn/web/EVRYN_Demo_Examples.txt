HOW TO USE THE MINIMAL INTERFACE DURING A LIVE DEMONSTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Opening the Workspace:
   Open http://localhost:8000 in any modern web browser. You will see an empty
   pure white canvas with subtle, generative black line structures drifting in
   the background.

2. Query Input:
   At the visual centre is a borderless text input. Click or start typing.
   - Press Enter to submit your text for causal extraction.
   - Press Shift+Enter to insert a newline for multi-line or multi-paragraph input.
   - The input smoothly moves to the top, revealing the interactive causal graph.

3. Interactive Graph Navigation:
   - Click and drag any node to explore its causal connections. Force-directed
     physics and collision avoidance maintain layout stability.
   - Click a node to view its canonical concept, in-degree, and out-degree.
   - Click an edge to inspect the grounded evidence, causal verb/connective,
     document provenance, and confidence score.
   - Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag empty canvas to pan.

4. The Plus Control (+):
   - The square black plus control appears next to the input and at the bottom
     of completed results.
   - Click it to upload PDF or TXT documents, or load pre-configured demo examples.
   - New inputs incrementally extend the current session without erasing
     prior evidence or overwriting contradictions.

5. Session Management:
   - Open the discreet left sidebar to browse previous sessions.
   - Click an entry to restore its exact graph, layout, and provenance.
   - Click the small 'x' next to a session to delete it from persistent SQLite storage.

[Title]: Direct Receptor Activation
[Text to Paste]:
Epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) activation directly stimulates the downstream MAPK pathway.

[What it Tests]:
Single-sentence, unambiguous direct causal regulation with explicit directionality. Tests detection of canonical biomedical entities ("EGFR", "MAPK") and active causal verb phrasing ("stimulates").

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- EGFR -> stimulates (UPREGULATES) -> MAPK

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 2: MULTI-HOP CAUSAL CHAIN
================================================================================
[Title]: Sequential Transcriptional Signaling Cascade
[Text to Paste]:
Interleukin-6 activates STAT3 phosphorylation. Subsequently, activated STAT3 stimulates the transcription of SOCS3, which downregulates JAK kinase activity.

[What it Tests]:
Detection of sequential causal dependencies (A -> B -> C -> D) within connected discourse. Verifies that the graph builder preserves intermediate mediation nodes and distinguishes upregulation from downregulation.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- IL-6 -> activates (UPREGULATES) -> STAT3
- STAT3 -> stimulates (UPREGULATES) -> SOCS3
- SOCS3 -> downregulates (DOWNREGULATES) -> JAK

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Without coreference resolution, "activated STAT3" might be instantiated as an isolated node distinct from "STAT3". Transitive pruning must not collapse the chain (IL-6 -> JAK) into a direct edge without marking it as inferred.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 3: SCIENTIFIC MECHANISM (HYPOXIA PATHWAY)
================================================================================
[Title]: Oxygen Sensing & Angiogenic Feedback
[Text to Paste]:
Under hypoxic stress, cellular Hypoxia-Inducible Factor 1-alpha (HIF-1alpha) is stabilized and drives the transcription of VEGF. In turn, elevated VEGF promotes tumor angiogenesis, while Von Hippel-Lindau tumor suppressor (VHL) promotes the ubiquitin-mediated degradation of HIF-1alpha under normoxic conditions.

[What it Tests]:
Biochemical mechanisms involving conditional regulation, passive constructions ("is stabilized"), and opposing regulatory forces (VEGF promoting angiogenesis vs VHL degrading HIF-1alpha).

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- Hypoxic stress -> stabilizes (UPREGULATES) -> HIF-1alpha
- HIF-1alpha -> drives (UPREGULATES) -> VEGF
- VEGF -> promotes (UPREGULATES) -> Angiogenesis
- VHL -> degrades (DOWNREGULATES) -> HIF-1alpha

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Complex noun phrases ("ubiquitin-mediated degradation") require syntactic head extraction to prevent bloated node labels. Environmental conditions ("under normoxic conditions") should be captured as context rather than independent nodes.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 4: NEGATION AND SPECULATION
================================================================================
[Title]: Negative Control & Speculative Regulation
[Text to Paste]:
Administration of Compound X did not induce p53 phosphorylation in wild-type cells. We hypothesize that elevated ROS might activate Caspase-3 under prolonged oxidative stress.

[What it Tests]:
The ability of the pipeline to filter out negated claims or classify them as REFUTED_NEGATION rather than asserting a positive causal edge. Verifies that speculative modal verbs ("hypothesize", "might") produce lower confidence or HYPOTHESIS_CLAIM tags.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- Compound X -/-> does NOT induce (REFUTED_NEGATION) -> p53
- ROS -> might activate (HYPOTHESIS_CLAIM / speculative) -> Caspase-3

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Naive co-occurrence baselines will falsely assert Compound X -> p53. Speculative claims may be assigned excessive confidence if hedge lexicons are not consulted.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 5: MULTI-PARAGRAPH CAUSAL MECHANISM
================================================================================
[Title]: Cellular Energetics and Autophagy Induction
[Text to Paste]:
Severe glucose deprivation triggers the rapid activation of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) through phosphorylation by upstream kinase LKB1.

Once activated, AMPK directly phosphorylates and activates the TSC2 complex, thereby inhibiting mammalian target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1).

The inhibition of mTORC1 liberates ULK1 from repressive phosphorylation, ultimately promoting macroautophagy and cellular survival.

[What it Tests]:
Extracting and connecting causal assertions across distinct paragraphs. Tests paragraph segmentation, canonical entity matching across paragraphs (AMPK, mTORC1, ULK1), and causal polarity tracking.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- LKB1 -> activates (UPREGULATES) -> AMPK
- Glucose deprivation -> triggers (UPREGULATES) -> AMPK
- AMPK -> activates (UPREGULATES) -> TSC2
- TSC2 -> inhibits (DOWNREGULATES) -> mTORC1
- mTORC1 -> represses (DOWNREGULATES) -> ULK1
- ULK1 -> promotes (UPREGULATES) -> Autophagy

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Paragraph breaks can cause fragment loss in systems that only evaluate single sentences. Multi-step cascades risk compound directional inversion if passive phrases ("liberates ... from repressive") are misparsed.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 6: CROSS-SECTION PAPER RECONSTRUCTION
================================================================================
[Title]: Multi-Section Inflammatory Signaling
[Text to Paste]:
[Section: Introduction]
Tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNF-alpha) is a master proinflammatory cytokine known to stimulate the nuclear factor kappa B (NF-kB) signaling pathway.

[Section: Results]
Treatment of primary macrophages with recombinant TNF-alpha led to rapid IKK complex phosphorylation. Nuclear translocation of NF-kB subsequently induced transcription of interleukin-6 (IL-6).

[Section: Discussion]
Our findings indicate that sustained NF-kB activation is essential for IL-6 secretion, whereas pharmacological inhibition of IKK suppresses downstream inflammatory cytokine production.

[What it Tests]:
Cross-section provenance tracking. Verifies that the extractor records section titles ("Introduction", "Results", "Discussion"), preserves source offsets, and handles repeated entity mentions across document sections.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- TNF-alpha -> stimulates (UPREGULATES) -> NF-kB
- TNF-alpha -> led to phosphorylation of (UPREGULATES) -> IKK
- NF-kB -> induced (UPREGULATES) -> IL-6
- IKK inhibition -> suppresses (DOWNREGULATES) -> IL-6

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Duplicate assertions across sections (e.g., TNF-alpha -> NF-kB mentioned in both Introduction and Results) should be deduplicated while accumulating all supporting citations rather than creating parallel redundant edges.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 7: MULTIPLE CAUSES AND MULTIPLE EFFECTS
================================================================================
[Title]: Pleiotropic Wnt/beta-Catenin Network
[Text to Paste]:
Extracellular Wnt3a ligand binding recruits Dishevelled (Dvl), which inhibits the Axin/GSK3-beta destruction complex. Consequently, stabilized beta-catenin translocates to the nucleus, where it activates both Cyclin D1 and c-Myc expression, driving both cell cycle progression and metabolic reprogramming.

[What it Tests]:
Forking (one cause -> multiple effects) and fan-in (multiple causes -> single effect) graph topologies. Evaluates entity boundary detection when multiple coordination tokens ("both ... and") are present.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- Wnt3a -> recruits (UPREGULATES) -> Dishevelled
- Dishevelled -> inhibits (DOWNREGULATES) -> GSK3-beta
- GSK3-beta inhibition -> stabilizes (UPREGULATES) -> beta-catenin
- beta-catenin -> activates (UPREGULATES) -> Cyclin D1
- beta-catenin -> activates (UPREGULATES) -> c-Myc
- Cyclin D1 / c-Myc -> drives (UPREGULATES) -> Cell cycle progression
- Cyclin D1 / c-Myc -> drives (UPREGULATES) -> Metabolic reprogramming

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Coordination structures ("both A and B") frequently result in concatenated entity strings (e.g., "Cyclin D1 and c-Myc" extracted as a single chimeric entity) unless entity normalization parses conjoined noun phrases.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 8: AMBIGUOUS CORRELATION VS CAUSATION
================================================================================
[Title]: Confounding & Non-Causal Association Challenge
[Text to Paste]:
Serum biomarker Protein Alpha was found to be strongly correlated with Patient Age (r = 0.82, p < 0.001). However, multivariate regression demonstrated that advanced age does not cause elevated Protein Alpha; rather, chronic systemic inflammation independently increases both aging markers and Protein Alpha levels.

[What it Tests]:
Adversarial test for discriminating observational correlation from true causality. Verifies that the model does NOT create an edge for "Protein Alpha <-> Patient Age", recognizes explicit refutation ("does not cause"), and extracts the true common cause ("Chronic inflammation").

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- Patient Age -/-> does not cause (REFUTED_NEGATION) -> Protein Alpha
- Chronic inflammation -> increases (UPREGULATES) -> Aging markers
- Chronic inflammation -> increases (UPREGULATES) -> Protein Alpha

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Weak NLP pipelines frequently misidentify "correlated with" as a causal relation. High false-positive risk if the system lacks correlation filtering.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 9: EVIDENCE GROUNDING VS UNSUPPORTED TRANSITIVITY
================================================================================
[Title]: Strict Grounding vs Transitive Reachability
[Text to Paste]:
Transforming growth factor beta (TGF-beta) triggers SMAD3 phosphorylation. In a separate assay, high SMAD3 levels induced Fibronectin synthesis.

[What it Tests]:
Demonstrating EVRYN's grounding distinction between directly supported causal claims and hypothetical multi-hop transitive leaps. The direct edges have explicit sentence-level textual evidence. A direct edge from TGF-beta to Fibronectin is NOT stated in the text and must NOT be asserted as a factual extraction.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- TGF-beta -> triggers (UPREGULATES) -> SMAD3  [Directly Grounded]
- SMAD3 -> induced (UPREGULATES) -> Fibronectin  [Directly Grounded]

[Unsupported / Inferred Leap]:
- TGF-beta -> Fibronectin [TRANSITIVE / INFERRED ONLY - Must not be fabricated as a direct assertion]

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Over-eager graph completion algorithms often inject transitive shortcuts into the extracted graph. EVRYN preserves evidence grounding by keeping edges strictly tied to source sentences.

DEMONSTRATION EXAMPLE 10: OPEN-DOMAIN CAUSAL REASONING
================================================================================
[Title]: Macroeconomic & Climatic Open-Domain Statements
[Text to Paste]:
High central bank interest rates increase borrowing costs, which dampens corporate capital expenditure and reduces overall consumer spending. Meanwhile, elevated atmospheric carbon dioxide traps thermal radiation, driving global surface temperature anomalies.

[What it Tests]:
Open-domain causal reasoning beyond biomedical nomenclature. Tests generic noun phrase extraction, causal verb handling ("increase", "dampens", "reduces", "traps", "driving"), and dual disjoint causal components in a single session.

[Explicitly Stated Relations]:
- Interest rates -> increase (UPREGULATES) -> Borrowing costs
- Borrowing costs -> dampens (DOWNREGULATES) -> Capital expenditure
- Borrowing costs -> reduces (DOWNREGULATES) -> Consumer spending
- Carbon dioxide -> traps (UPREGULATES) -> Thermal radiation
- Thermal radiation -> driving (UPREGULATES) -> Global temperature anomalies

[Limitations & Expected Failure Modes]:
Pipelines locked strictly to medical ontologies (e.g., MeSH/UMLS/Gene Ontology) fail completely on open-domain topics. EVRYN's general syntactic dependency and pattern matcher enables general-purpose extraction while preserving scientific rigor.
