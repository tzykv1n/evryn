## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 1.7 tackles the single largest remaining failure mode in biomedical causal relation extraction identified during the Phase 1.6 error audit: **discourse-level claims spanning sentence boundaries**.

In Phase 1.6, out of 48 total false negatives across the 20-paper benchmark, **28 false negatives (58.3%)** were categorized as discourse-level claims whose entities do not co-occur within any single sentence span of `doc.sentences`. 

### Key Contributions & Research Findings

1. **Linguistic Decomposition of Discourse False Negatives:**
   An exhaustive census of all 28 discourse-level false negatives revealed that they fall into four distinct computational mechanisms:
   - *Sentence Boundary Truncation Artifacts (7.1%):* Taxonomic abbreviations (`P.`, `S.`, `E. coli`) prematurely split by regex sentence tokenizers.
   - *Demonstrative Causal Anaphora (17.9%):* Causal pronouns (`"This phenotype accounts for..."`, `"These findings explain..."`) linking adjacent sentences ($dist = 1$).
   - *Synonym, Operon, & Character Variant Mismatches (46.4%):* Co-occurring in the same or adjacent sentence, but missed by exact-string matching due to Greek unicode (`σE` vs `sigmaE`) or operon naming (`arnBCADTEF` for `arn Operon`).
   - *Multi-Paragraph Experimental Synthesis (28.6%):* High-level scientific conclusions drawn across distant paragraphs without local syntactic triggers.

2. **Bounded Document-Context Resolver (`DocumentContextResolver`):**
   Implemented in `evryn/paper/document_context.py`, featuring:
   - Demonstrative causal anaphora resolution connecting antecedent causal effects to subsequent phenotypic outcomes.
   - Adjacent-sentence explicit discourse connective tracking (`Consequently`, `Therefore`, `As a result`).
   - Strict biological invariant guards (terminal phenotypes cannot cause molecular regulators; environmental inputs cannot be causal targets; complementation markers excluded).

3. **Controlled Experimental Matrix (E0 through E5):**
   Evaluated across all 20 benchmark papers partitioned into 4 strictly isolated splits:
   - **E3 (Demonstrative Causal Anaphora)** achieves the optimal precision/recall trade-off:
     - **Dev Direct F1 increases from 0.5076/0.5133 to 0.5285** (95% CI: `[0.4722, 0.5875]`).
     - **Dev Recall increases from 63.89% to 66.67%** (recovering gold discourse claims).
     - **Zero regression on Locked Test (Direct F1 = 0.3429)** and **External V2 (Direct F1 = 0.3289)**.
   - **Empirical Warning on Sentence Stitching (E4):** Unconstrained abbreviation stitching without syntactic parsing caused precision loss (Dev Direct F1 dropped to 0.4927 due to 3 additional false positives). Consequently, sentence stitching was ablated and disabled in production defaults.

## 4. Provenance and Grounding Integrity

Every assertion generated by `DocumentContextResolver` adheres to EVRYN's strict evidence standards:
1. `evidence_sentence_ids`: Contains both sentence identifiers `[S_k, S_{k+1}]`.
2. `evidence_quote`: Conjoined verbatim span of both sentences.
3. `antecedent_quote`: Explicitly preserves the antecedent text from sentence $S_k$.
4. `discourse_relation`: Marked as `"demonstrative_anaphora"` or `"causal_connective"`.
5. `char_span`: Exact character start of $S_k$ to character end of $S_{k+1}$.
